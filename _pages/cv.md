Scientific Skills
======
**Formal genetics**: genetic analysis by crosses, generation of new *Drosophila melanogaster* lines, genetic screening and phenotypic observations under the stereomicroscope.

**Cytogenetics**: preparation and analysis of karyotype, cell cycle and possible chromosomal aberrations in *Drosophila melanogaster*; micronucleus analysis and comet assay *in vitro* and *in vivo*, detection of apoptotic nuclei.

**Imaging**: use of transmitted light, fluorescence and inversion microscopy; live imaging of viable cells (Nanolive); image analysis using ImageJ, Fiji and CaspLab.

**Molecular and cellular biology**: cell cultures (immortalized and tumoral lines), cell viability assays and growth curves; extraction of DNA, RNA and proteins from cells and from *Drosophila* and mice tissues, Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR); reverse transcription, Real Time-PCR, gene-knockdown by RNA-interference in *Drosophila* lines; extraction of ancient genomic and mitochondrial DNA from archaeological findings, DNA fragmentation analysis; immunofluorescence.

**Pharmacology and toxicology**: genetic, molecular and phenotypic analyses of the effect of compounds and nanomaterials *in vitro* and *in vivo* (*Drosophila melanogaster*).
