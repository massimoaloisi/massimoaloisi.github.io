Scientific Skills
======
<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<strong>Formal genetics</strong>: genetic analysis by crosses, generation of new <em>Drosophila melanogaster</em> lines, genetic screening and phenotypic observations under the stereomicroscope. </p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<strong>Cytogenetics</strong>: preparation and analysis of karyotype, cell cycle and possible chromosomal aberrations in <em>Drosophila melanogaster</em>; micronucleus analysis and comet assay <em>in vitro</em> and <em>in vivo</em>, detection of apoptotic nuclei. </p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<strong>Imaging</strong>: use of transmitted light, fluorescence and inversion microscopy; live imaging of viable cells (Nanolive); image analysis using ImageJ, Fiji and CaspLab. </p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<strong>Molecular and cell biology</strong>: cell cultures (immortalized and tumoral lines), cell viability assays and growth curves; extraction of DNA, RNA and proteins from cells and from <em>Drosophila</em> and mice tissues, Polymerase Chain Reaction (PCR); reverse transcription, Real Time-PCR, gene-knockdown by RNA-interference in <em>Drosophila</em> lines; extraction of ancient genomic and mitochondrial DNA from archaeological findings, DNA fragmentation analysis; immunofluorescence, FACS. </p>

<p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<strong>Pharmacology and toxicology</strong>: genetic, molecular and phenotypic analyses of the effect of compounds and nanomaterials <em>in vitro</em> and <em>in vivo</em> (<em>Drosophila melanogaster</em>). </p>
